Machine Learning Engineer - NLP & Generative AI
Nimbus Talent Labs (fictional company) - Paris, hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
Nimbus Talent Labs builds AI tools that help recruiters and candidates. We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to design, build and run our LLM-powered features, from retrieval pipelines to production APIs.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design and implement retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines over large document collections.
- Build and maintain embedding models and vector search infrastructure.
- Deploy ML services to production on AWS and monitor their quality, latency and cost.
- Evaluate LLM outputs with offline test sets and human feedback.
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to turn user needs into ML features.

REQUIREMENTS
- 3+ years of professional experience in machine learning or data science.
- Strong Python skills and experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, prompt engineering and Hugging Face Transformers.
- Experience with vector databases or similarity search (FAISS, Pinecone, pgvector...).
- Experience building REST APIs (FastAPI or Flask) and containerising services with Docker.
- Experience deploying models on AWS (SageMaker, Lambda or ECS).
- Good communication skills in English.

NICE TO HAVE
- Kubernetes and Terraform.
- MLOps tooling such as MLflow.
- Experience mentoring junior engineers.
- French is a plus.

WHAT WE OFFER
Hybrid work, learning budget, and a friendly team of 25 people.

# AI Job Market & Interview Coach

Compare a CV with a job description and get:
- an explained match score;
- how often the job market asks for your missing skills (787,923 analysed postings);
- personalised interview questions with grounded feedback.

**How to try it:** click **Load sample documents** (a fictional CV and job), then **Analyze match**.

**Privacy:** documents are processed in memory and sent to the configured LLM provider. Please use fictional or anonymised CVs.

Source code, data pipeline, statistics, ML and documentation: <https://github.com/Raphzaf/ai-job-interview-coach>
